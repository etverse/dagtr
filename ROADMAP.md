# dagtr roadmap

dagtr will turn a directed acyclic graph into an adjustment set and a `confounders` formula for the other etverse packages, as a thin layer over dagitty. The [etverse design](https://etverse.github.io/design.html) fixes its scope and interface. Its methods are chosen from the literature before the first code, and each planned item will be a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/etverse/dagtr/issues).

## Not planned here

- Estimation: causatr and the packages built on it. Graph drawing and testing beyond what dagitty offers.
